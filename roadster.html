A True Pilot's Car

Roadster can perform at levels unlike any other production car. It goes beyond Plaid to unlock an entirely new dimension of driving. It challenges the limits of design, engineering and physics to deliver an expertly crafted extra-terrestrial vehicle.

Hidden Aero

Designed to be minimalist yet highly technical. Hidden headlights, a full-length taillight and flush-mounted aerodynamics conceal the vehicle's true form. It is grounded by super light wheels with thin spokes that reinforce its structural strength when earthbound.

Ano White

Ano White paint takes inspiration from aerospace techniques that provide UV and cryogenic protection when exposed to the harshness of space, plus electrostatic dissipation and corrosion resistance to protect rockets during pre-launch and recovery.

Gold Glazed

Tinted glass windows are designed to mimic the copper coating that gives spacesuit visors their gold radiance. Subtle titanium accents further embody space-age design while accentuating the vehicle's most unique features.

Supersonic

Our iconic Ultrawhite interior is accented with gray microsuede detailing. Seats feature microsuede bolsters for added grip. Yoke is designed specifically for steer by wire. A center touchscreen supported by two smaller forward displays provides critical driving info.

Mass Control

First principles engineering, manual controls and a fixed seat position help reduce overall vehicle mass. With lightweight, high-strength materials throughout, including magnesium, titanium and carbon fiber, we are able to achieve even greater reductions. This allows Roadster to respond to driver inputs almost instantly during extra-dimensional driving.

Out of This World

We already put Roadster in a rocket. Now we put a rocket in Roadster. We created a vehicle capable of next-level performance. Additional SpaceX components can transform that road performance into a truly revolutionary experience.

Beyond The Limits of Physics

Our multi-mode fan and ground sealing system acts as a downforce generator, pulling air out from beneath the vehicle. This provides enough suction to hold the vehicle upside down, even when stationary. Powered fans and active wings maximize traction and cornering speeds in all conditions, achieving massive downforce at zero speed. A next-gen steer-by-wire system offers incredible feedback in various road conditions. Activate Track Mode to lower the car and transform the suspension. High downforce or low drag modes can then be used as needed on the track.¹

Gravity Defying

Advanced cold-gas thrusters use all-electric actuators to offer precise thrust, completing micro adjustments within a fraction of a second. This helps stabilize the vehicle in flight.¹
