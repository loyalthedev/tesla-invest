Vehicle Warranty

New Vehicle Limited Warranty

Your new Tesla vehicle is protected by a New Vehicle Limited Warranty, which includes the Basic Vehicle Limited Warranty, the Supplemental Restraint System Limited Warranty, the Battery and Drive Unit Limited Warranty, the Body Rust Limited Warranty and the Zero-Emissions Vehicle Limited Warranties. Select a model below to view the current New Vehicle Limited Warranty:

  • Model S
  • Model 3
  • Model X
  • Model Y
  • Cybertruck

Note: This warranty should only be used for general knowledge. For the authoritative information of your vehicle, view the warranty provided with your vehicle.

Basic Vehicle Limited Warranty

The Basic Vehicle Limited Warranty covers your vehicle for 4 years or 50,000 miles, whichever comes first.

Supplemental Restraint System Limited Warranty

The Supplemental Restraint System Limited Warranty covers your vehicle for 5 years or 60,000 miles, whichever comes first.

Body Rust Limited Warranty

The Body Rust Limited Warranty covers rust perforation (hole through the vehicle’s body panel from the inside outward) resulting from a defect in material or workmanship. The Body Rust Limited Warranty covers your vehicle for 12 years and unlimited miles.

Note: For vehicles purchased before February 1, 2019, there is no Body Rust Limited Warranty coverage.

Zero-Emissions Vehicle (ZEV) Limited Warranties

If your New Vehicle Limited Warranty is valid in the United States or Canada, your vehicle may be covered by the ZEV Limited Warranties. The ZEV Limited Warranties largely overlap with other coverages in the New Vehicle Limited Warranty. For more details on all four of these warranties, review the ‘ZEV Limited Warranties’ section of your New Vehicle Limited Warranty. One of the ZEV Limited Warranties is the High-Priced Propulsion-Related Parts ZEV Limited Warranty.

High-Priced Propulsion-Related Parts ZEV Limited Warranty

The High-Priced Propulsion-Related Parts ZEV Limited Warranty covers the repair or replacement necessary to correct defects in the materials or workmanship of High-Priced Propulsion-Related Parts that reach a certain pricing threshold. Eligible vehicles are covered by the High-Priced Propulsion-Related Parts ZEV Limited Warranty for a period of 7 years or 70,000 miles, whichever comes first. For further details, including the list of eligible vehicle parts, review your New Vehicle Limited Warranty.

Emissions Performance Warranty

If your New Vehicle Limited Warranty is valid in the United States, Canada or Puerto Rico, and your vehicle is model year 2027 or later, your vehicle may be covered by the Emissions Performance Warranty. Available for Model 3 and Model Y vehicles, the Emissions Performance Warranty covers the repair or replacement of components that are needed to charge the high voltage battery, store energy or transmit power to move the vehicle. Eligible vehicles are covered for a period of 8 years or 80,000 miles, whichever comes first. For further details, including the list of eligible vehicle parts, review your New Vehicle Limited Warranty.

Pre-Owned Vehicle Limited Warranty

The Pre-Owned Vehicle Limited Warranty gives additional peace of mind to owners of Certified Pre-Owned vehicles purchased directly from Tesla.

Tesla Certified Pre-Owned vehicles are covered by the remainder of 4 years or 50,000 miles left on the Basic Vehicle Limited Warranty. After expiration, the Pre-Owned Vehicle Limited Warranty provides additional coverage of 1 year or 10,000 miles. If the Basic Vehicle Limited Warranty has already expired, the Pre-Owned Vehicle Limited Warranty will provide coverage of 1 year or 10,000 miles, starting from your delivery date.

Note: If you are leasing a vehicle from Tesla, then the Pre-Owned Vehicle Limited Warranty will be up to the term or original mileage stated in the lease, whichever comes first, instead of the 1 year or 10,000 miles referenced above. All other terms remain the same.

The balance of original Battery and Drive Unit Limited Warranty still applies for Certified Pre-Owned vehicles.

For further details, review the Pre-Owned Vehicle Limited Warranty.

Parts, Body and Paint Repair Limited Warranty

The Tesla Parts, Body & Paint Repair Limited Warranty covers parts purchased directly from Tesla, over the counter, online or purchased and installed by Tesla Service or a Tesla Collision Center.

This limited warranty covers the repair or replacement necessary to remedy any “failures” that are determined by Tesla to have occurred under normal use.

“Failure” means the complete failure or inability of a covered part to perform the function(s) for which it was designed, due to defects in material or workmanship of the part manufactured or supplied by Tesla, which occur under normal use. Failure does not include the gradual loss in operating performance due to normal wear and tear.

The Tesla Parts, Body & Paint Repair Limited Warranty begins on the purchase date of the part(s), and coverage extends for a period of 12 months or 12,500 miles, whichever comes first. However, some specific categories of parts have different warranty coverage periods:

  • Sheet metal: Limited lifetime
  • Drive Unit1: 4 years or 50,000 miles, whichever comes first
  • Vehicle High Voltage Battery1: 4 years or 50,000 miles, whichever comes first
  • Wall Connectors: 4 years for personal use, 1 year for commercial use
  • Touchscreen and Media Control Unit: 2 years or 25,000 miles, whichever comes first
  • Clear Protection Film2: 10 years
  • Color Paint Film2: 5 years
  • Vinyl Wrap: 1 year or 12,500 miles, whichever comes first

For full details, review the Tesla Parts, Body & Paint Repair Limited Warranty.

Optional Extended Service Agreement

If you have a Model S, Model X, Model 3 or Model Y, you may be eligible for an Extended Service Agreement. The Extended Service Agreement takes effect on the date that your original New Vehicle Limited Warranty expires.

Optional High Voltage Battery and Drive Unit Extended Service Agreement

If you have a Model 3 or Model Y, you may be eligible for the High Voltage Battery and Drive Unit Extended Service Agreement (Battery ESA). The Battery ESA takes effect on the date that your Battery and Drive Unit Limited Warranty expires.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to take my vehicle to the Tesla Service Center?

To receive service covered by a Tesla limited warranty, you will need to take your vehicle to a Tesla Service Center. However, for any non-warranty repair or maintenance, you may use a Tesla Service Center or visit your preferred non-Tesla vehicle service center. Tesla is not responsible for any issues caused by improper repair or maintenance by a third-party service center. Learn more about the different service options available and how to schedule service in the Tesla app.

Does the New Vehicle Limited Warranty transfer to the new owner if I sell my vehicle?

Yes. Your New Vehicle Limited Warranty will follow your vehicle and be transferred to the new owner when a vehicle ownership transfer is performed through Tesla.

How do I know if repairs performed during a service appointment are covered by my vehicle’s limited warranty?

Before your service appointment, the Service team will review your appointment details, perform remote diagnostics and prepare an initial estimate for your upcoming visit. Review the estimate in the Tesla app by tapping ‘Service’ > ‘View estimate.’ Repairs that are expected to be covered under warranty should appear as $0 on the estimate.

Certain repairs require an in-person inspection before the Service team can confirm warranty coverage. These repairs may appear on your estimate with an initial cost. If these repairs qualify for warranty coverage, the Service team will verify this on the date of your appointment and any charges for those repairs will be removed. For any questions about warranty coverage or repair costs, you can contact the Service team directly through the Tesla app.

Note: You will be notified of any changes to the initial estimate. Service will not be performed without your approval of any updated costs.

Who should I contact for any questions or disputes about my Vehicle Limited Warranty?

Tesla asks that you first provide us, during the applicable warranty period specified in the New Vehicle Limited Warranty or Pre-Owned Vehicle Limited Warranty, written notification of any defects you have experienced within a reasonable time and allow Tesla an opportunity to make any needed repairs before you pursue any remedy under the applicable laws in your jurisdiction. For further details, including information on our Dispute Resolution Program, review the New Vehicle Limited Warranty or Pre-Owned Vehicle Limited Warranty.

For California only, a consumer shall provide written notification of any noncompliance with the applicable express limited warranty by email to CAwarranty@tesla.com or by certified or registered mail, return receipt requested, to Tesla, Inc. c/o CT Corporation, 330 N Brand Boulevard, Suite 700, Glendale, CA 91203, if requesting a repurchase or replacement of a vehicle. See the New Vehicle Limited Warranty or Pre-Owned Vehicle Limited Warranty for more information and your obligations.

Where can I find the warranty coverage that comes with my vehicle?

The limited warranties linked above should only be used for general knowledge. For the authoritative information that is related to your vehicle, you can reference the limited warranty provided with your vehicle.

Note: The limited warranties above apply only to vehicles purchased directly from Tesla on or after the date listed on the limited warranty; any Tesla vehicles purchased prior to that date are subject to the applicable limited warranty effective as of the date of purchase of such vehicle directly from Tesla.

You can access certain details about your limited warranty coverage in the Tesla app by following these steps:

  1. Open the Tesla app.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of the Tesla app home screen and select ‘Specs & Warranty.’
  3. Tap the ‘Warranty’ tab to view the warranty coverage for your vehicle.
Does my Tesla vehicle’s limited warranty cover damage caused by a third-party bi-directional vehicle adaptor or charger?

No. Your vehicle’s limited warranty does not cover damage caused by third-party vehicle adaptors or chargers. For a full list of what is not covered by the limited warranty, review the Exclusions and Limitations section of the limited warranty provided with your vehicle.
