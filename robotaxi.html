This Is What Real Autonomy Looks Like

Do what you want with the time you would have spent driving.

Stream your favorite movies and TV shows in Tesla Theater or by automatically signing into your preferred streaming services through your Robotaxi account.

Browse our collection of Tesla Arcade games and play while you ride. Bring along a Bluetooth controller for upgraded gaming on longer rides.

Take meetings on the touchscreen, catch up on emails and work on your laptop using your own hotspot.2

No Wheel, Pedals or Driver

Fully autonomous means there’s no need for a steering wheel, pedals or mirrors. Full autonomy means you get the car all to yourself.2

Fully Customizable

Your ride arrives ready with your climate, seat position and entertainment preferences loaded according to your Robotaxi preferences.2

Tons of Storage

The trunk is large enough to fit 4 suitcases, over 15 bags of groceries or 20 cubic feet of anything else you want to bring along.2

Designed for Accessibility

Our vehicles have space for service animals and assistive devices.3 The app is screen-reader compatible and is available in 29 languages with more being added.

Engineered for Safety
